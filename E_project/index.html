Innovations & Engineering

Mastery of Precision Technology

Every timepiece at Alberto Watch Company represents centuries of horological evolution coupled with contemporary metallurgical and micro-mechanical breakthroughs.

Superlative Chronometer & Escapement

Equipped with paramagnetic blue Parachrom hairsprings and Chronergy escapements insensitive to magnetic fields, maintaining precision within -2/+2 seconds per day.

Cerachrom Ceramic Bezels

Virtually scratchproof monobloc ceramic bezels impervious to ultraviolet discoloration, engraved with 24-karat gold or platinum PVD graduation numerals.

300m Hydrostatic Immersion

Tested to 300 metres (1,000 feet) with double O-ring seals, reinforced sapphire crystals, and precision screw-down crowns.

Citizen Eco-Drive Solar Engine

Converts any artificial or natural light into stored electrical power, eliminating battery replacements forever while preserving clean quartz atomic synchronization.

Openworked Skeleton Craft

Twin-barrel architectures delivering up to 72 hours of uninterrupted power reserve, with 28 synthetic rubies and hand-chamfered bridges.

CuSn8 Marine Bronze & GMT

Organic patina development on solid bronze aviation casings paired with independent 24-hour jumping hands for transcontinental flight navigators.
